Role Overview

Clueso is looking for a Frontend-Heavy Full Stack Engineer to build and own its AI-powered Articles & SOP Editor. You'll develop rich document editing experiences, AI-assisted workflows, reusable content systems, and collaborate directly with founders and designers to shape the product.
This role emphasizes frontend engineering while also requiring ownership of backend APIs and data models when needed.

- AI rewriting

Full Stack Development

Templates & Content Systems
